## The Five Rules of a High-Converting Bio Page

### Rule 1: Fewer Links Get More Clicks

This sounds counterintuitive but the data is clear. More choices cause people to choose nothing.

| Number of links on your page | Average click rate |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 links | 38% |
| 4 to 6 links | 29% |
| 7 to 10 links | 21% |
| More than 10 | 14% |

Keep your bio page to a maximum of 5 links. Put your most important one at the very top where it is visible without scrolling. That top position gets roughly 60% of all clicks on the page regardless of what the other links say.

### Rule 2: Native App Converts 2.5x Better Than a Browser

A user who opens your link inside your native app converts at 5.4% on average. A user stuck in Instagram's built-in browser converts at 2.1%.

The reasons are straightforward:
- Apple Pay and Google Pay work in native apps, not in built-in browsers
- The user is already logged in to your app
- Native checkout flows have fewer steps
- You can ask for push notification permission after purchase

A smart bio link checks whether someone has your app installed and sends them straight into the app if they do.

### Rule 3: Page Speed Is a Conversion Factor

Every extra second your bio link page takes to load cuts your conversion rate by up to 20%. If your link page is slow, people leave before they even see your links. Your page should load in under 500ms on a mobile connection.

### Rule 4: You Are Losing Attribution Data Right Now

Here is a problem that affects almost every creator and brand running campaigns. When someone taps your Instagram bio link with a UTM tag like `?utm_source=instagram`, then gets redirected to the App Store to install your app, that UTM tag disappears. The purchase that happens after install gets attributed to "direct" or "organic" in your analytics, not to the Instagram post that actually drove it.

This is called attribution loss and it is extremely common. A smart link router fixes it by storing the UTM parameters at the moment of the tap and delivering them into the app on first launch — a process called deferred deep linking.

When you set this up, you recover 60 to 80% of the conversion attribution that was previously being lost.

### Rule 5: Small Copy Changes Make a Big Difference

Most creators set up their bio page once and never touch it again. But the words on your buttons have a huge impact on whether people tap them.

| Weak copy | Stronger copy |
|---|---|
| "Click here" | "Get 20% off your first order" |
| "My shop" | "Shop the collection (closes Sunday)" |
| "Download the app" | "Get the app — free forever" |
| "Subscribe" | "Weekly tips — join 4,200 readers" |

Specificity and a sense of urgency consistently outperform generic labels. Test one change at a time and give each version two weeks of data before deciding.

## How Smart Bio Link Routing Works

When someone taps your bio link, a smart router runs through a series of checks in under 200 milliseconds and sends the person to the best possible destination. Here is the full decision tree:

```
User taps your bio link
        |
Smart router checks:
  - What device are they on? (iOS, Android, Desktop)
  - Are they inside a social app browser? (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
  - Is your app installed on their device?
        |
Routing decision:
  iPhone + app installed       -> Opens your app directly to the right screen
  iPhone + no app              -> App Store page
  Android + app installed      -> Opens your app directly to the right screen
  Android + no app             -> Google Play Store
  TikTok or Instagram browser  -> Shows "Open in App" button first
  Desktop                      -> Your web landing page
        |
Attribution saved:
  - UTM parameters stored
  - Click logged with timestamp, device, and source
  - Deferred deep link saved for app install attribution
```

All of this happens automatically. The user just taps and ends up in the right place.

## How to Structure Your Bio Page

Order your links like this, from top to bottom:

1. **Your main CTA** — whatever drives your primary revenue or goal (app install, shop, booking)
2. **Latest content** — your newest product, video, or article, updated regularly
3. **Community** — newsletter signup, Discord, Patreon
4. **Social platforms** — your other social accounts
5. **Contact** — WhatsApp or email

The first link gets the most clicks regardless of what it says. Put your money-maker there.

## How Deferred Deep Linking Saves Your Attribution

Here is the full attribution chain when you have deferred deep linking set up correctly:

1. Someone taps your Instagram bio link — it has `?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=aug-launch`
2. Your smart router stores: source, campaign, timestamp, and device
3. The user taps through to the App Store and installs your app
4. On first launch, your app's SDK retrieves the stored parameters
5. When they make a purchase, it is correctly attributed to that specific Instagram campaign

Without this, step 5 shows up as "direct" in your analytics and you have no idea which of your posts actually drove revenue.
